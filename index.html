How we work

We join your team, build what's needed, and keep it running.

Most of our clients are in a different country from us, so we've learned to work the way you already do: your repositories, your tracker, your chat, and a daily overlap in hours agreed before the first sprint. You get a short written update every week, a demo every sprint, and a founder on the call when it matters.

We build

Web platforms, APIs, mobile apps and the integrations between them, on Java, .NET, Node and React, with Kafka for events and Keycloak or Entra ID for sign-in. Built to your standards, reviewed before it ships.

We run

Cloud and DevOps on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud, with Kubernetes, Terraform and pipelines your team can read. QA wired into CI. Support and upgrades for the software you can't let age.

We secure

Security is part of every project, not a separate quote. Identity and access, hardening, and the tools you already run, from Palo Alto and Fortinet to CrowdStrike, Intune and Okta.

We add AI where it earns its place

We don't start with a model, we start with one workflow that costs your people time: answering from your documents, reading invoices and contracts, handling tickets and email. Then we build an assistant or agent for that one job, on OpenAI, Claude or Azure OpenAI, or on a local model if your data can't leave the building.

Agents with a human in the loop

Every agent we ship answers with sources, works only with the access its user has, and asks a person before doing anything that's hard to undo. We measure it against real cases before it goes live, and keep measuring after.

Plugged into what you already run

Your ERP, CRM, core banking and internal tools don't get rewritten. We connect through APIs, queues or the database, in Salesforce, SAP, Dynamics or ServiceNow, and the same team that built it keeps it monitored and maintained.

Process

From first call to first commit in four steps

Each step has a clear exit if it is not a fit.

Led by Besart Prebreza, Security and Process Lead

  1. Discovery call

    We learn your product, stack and the roles you need.

  2. Candidate profiles

    You review engineers and interview the ones you like.

  3. Trial sprint

    The team starts on a real task under NDA, with access limited to what the work needs. No long-term commitment yet.

  4. Steady delivery
